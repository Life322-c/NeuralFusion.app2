The challenge

The thinking challenges executives describe

Familiar patterns, offered as prompts for reflection rather than statistics.

01

Information is not the constraint

You often have the data. The harder part is weighing it against instinct, context and assumptions at the speed the decision demands.

02

Pressure narrows thinking

Under time pressure, many people fall back on one preferred way of thinking and stop checking it against the others.

03

Decisions are rarely reviewed

Important calls are made, then lost. Without a record and a review date, it is hard to learn from them.

04

AI can quietly replace judgement

Delegating thinking to tools can erode the habit of examining your own reasoning. Human cognitive agency is worth protecting on purpose.

The concept

Cognitive fragmentation

NeuralFusion uses this term for thinking that is disconnected, overloaded or stuck in a single mode, so the modes are not working together.

It is not a medical condition and not a measure of intelligence. It is a description of a pattern: a sharp analytical mind that ignores a persistent instinctive concern, or a fast intuitive decision-maker who never stops to examine assumptions.

Read more about cognitive fragmentation.

The aim is coordination, not balance. The CFI does not report a balance score, because whether balance relates to coordination is a question the data has to answer first.

The framework

The Four Thinking Modes Framework

Analytical

Examines information, logic, evidence and structure to support sound decisions.

Intuitive

Handles rapid pattern recognition and instinctive judgement that is not fully deliberate.

Associative

Connects ideas, generates relationships and lets concepts interact laterally.

Reflective

Examines experience, emotional influence, assumptions, decisions and meaning.

NeuralFusion does not treat these as four separate brains. The Four Thinking Modes Framework is a proprietary NeuralFusion model describing four modes of thinking that can interact within one person’s thinking. Read the full explanation.

Assessment

Your individual CFI assessment

One assessment, one place. The Executive Programme uses the same CFI as every other NeuralFusion user, so there is no second system to learn.

A 40-item self-report

The CFI asks how often a person does specific things when working on a problem or decision. It reflects a person’s own perception, not an independent observation.

Four mode scores and a Coordination score

Results are four thinking-mode scores plus a Coordination score, each from 0 to 100, with no overall total. A derived Fragmentation score is also shown.

Retake to see change

A retake shows the change in Coordination between attempts, so progress is measured against the person’s own baseline.

Early-stage and open about it

There are no bands or norms yet, and the instrument has not been independently validated. See the methodology page for scoring and limitations.

Method

The Core Loop

A four-stage routine you can apply to any problem, which becomes faster with practice.

  1. Decompose

    Break the problem into clear, separate parts.

  2. Switch modes

    Deliberately bring the mode best suited to each part.

  3. Synthesise

    Combine what each mode produced into one position.

  4. Stabilise

    Lock in the clarity and reduce the noise.

Practice

The Integration Protocol and Decision Room

The Core Loop applied to a live decision.

In the Decision Room you work a real decision through five steps: Decompose, Sense, Expand, Reflect, Fuse. You then save it, set a review date and record what happened.

NeuralFusion does not think for you. It organises and reflects your own words back to you.

  • Decisions are saved privately in your Decision Vault.
  • You can reopen a decision and record the outcome.
  • A decision reaches a team only if you choose to save it there.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the CFI a clinical or psychological test?

No. It is a proprietary self-report framework for reflecting on how you use and coordinate four ways of thinking. It is not a diagnosis and has not been independently validated. See the methodology page.

Does NeuralFusion tell me what to decide?

No. The Integration Protocol organises and reflects your own words back to you. It does not generate a recommendation or make the decision for you.

What do I actually do in the programme?

You take the CFI for a baseline, work through the lessons, use the Decision Room on real decisions, record outcomes, and retake the CFI to see change.

Is it free?

The CFI and the first lesson are free. The remaining lessons are part of the paid plan shown inside the app. Team packages exist for groups.

Can I run this with my leadership team?

Yes. Create a team and share one link, or send an enquiry if you want to talk through a small cohort. Team owners see aggregate results, never individual answers.

How long does it take?

The CFI takes about eight minutes. The lessons are 25 to 32 minutes each. The Decision Room is used as and when you have a real decision to work through.

Will my decisions be visible to anyone else?

Decisions are private to you unless you choose to save one to a team you belong to.
