Founder & Researcher

Life Edet

Founder of NeuralFusion™. Researcher focused on human cognitive agency, thinking, and cognitive performance in the age of artificial intelligence.

Life Edet is the founder of NeuralFusion™, an emerging platform for structured cognitive performance, and the author of the 2026 publication The Human Intelligence Imperative: Human Cognitive Agency in the Age of Artificial Intelligence.

Founder of NeuralFusion™

Life Edet founded NeuralFusion™ to address a specific problem: most people have never been taught how to think in a structured way, and the tools available to them measure output and productivity rather than the quality of the thinking behind it. NeuralFusion™ was built around two original frameworks developed by the founder, the Four Thinking Modes Framework™ and the Cognitive Fragmentation Index (CFI™), alongside the platform's Core Loop training method.

Research focus

Alongside building the NeuralFusion™ platform, the founder's research work examines human cognitive agency in an increasingly AI-mediated world: how people preserve independent judgment, structured reasoning, and decision ownership as AI systems take on more of the thinking work around them. This research is documented in the Research & Publications section.

Relationship between the research and the platform

The founder's research provides an intellectual foundation for NeuralFusion™'s broader philosophy, particularly its focus on preserving structured, independent human thinking. The NeuralFusion™ frameworks (the Four Thinking Modes Framework™ and the CFI™) are proprietary tools built by NeuralFusion™, developed alongside this research rather than as a direct output of it. NeuralFusion™ does not claim that the publication itself scientifically validates the platform's assessment methodology.
