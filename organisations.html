The challenge

These are patterns many leaders describe. They are framing questions for your own organisation, not statistics.

01

Strong individuals, fragmented thinking

Analysts, instinctive operators and creative thinkers sit in the same room, yet decisions can stall when those ways of thinking are not brought together on purpose.

02

Training that is hard to apply

Why it matters

Why cognitive integration matters

Cognitive integration is NeuralFusion’s name for deliberately coordinating several ways of thinking on one problem, instead of defaulting to the one a person or team prefers.

When a team works on a hard problem, the quality of the outcome depends on more than the information available. It also depends on whether the evidence, the instinctive concerns, the lateral options and the underlying assumptions all get a fair hearing.

NeuralFusion gives people a common vocabulary and a practice routine for doing that, and gives team owners an aggregate view of how the group reports its own thinking.

What this is not. NeuralFusion is a proprietary framework at an early stage. It is not a clinical tool, it does not diagnose anyone, and it is not designed for selection, hiring or performance appraisal. We would rather say this plainly than oversell it.

The framework

The Four Thinking Modes Framework

Analytical

Examines information, logic, evidence and structure to support sound decisions.

Intuitive

Handles rapid pattern recognition and instinctive judgement that is not fully deliberate.

Associative

Connects ideas, generates relationships and lets concepts interact laterally.

Reflective

Examines experience, emotional influence, assumptions, decisions and meaning.

NeuralFusion does not treat these as four separate brains. The Four Thinking Modes Framework is a proprietary NeuralFusion model describing four modes of thinking that can interact within one person’s thinking. Read the full explanation.

The assessment

The CFI assessment

The Cognitive Fragmentation Index (CFI) is the starting point for every person and team on the platform.

A 40-item self-report

The CFI asks how often a person does specific things when working on a problem or decision. It reflects a person’s own perception, not an independent observation.

Four mode scores and a Coordination score

Results are four thinking-mode scores plus a Coordination score, each from 0 to 100, with no overall total. A derived Fragmentation score is also shown.

Retake to see change

A retake shows the change in Coordination between attempts, so progress is measured against the person’s own baseline.

Early-stage and open about it

There are no bands or norms yet, and the instrument has not been independently validated. See the methodology page for scoring and limitations.

The Core Loop and Integration Protocol

The Core Loop is a four-stage routine: decompose a problem, switch deliberately between modes, synthesise, and stabilise. The Integration Protocol applies it to a live decision in the Decision Room, using the person’s own words. NeuralFusion organises and reflects people’s thinking back to them. It does not make the decision for them.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the CFI a clinically validated instrument?

No. The CFI is a proprietary NeuralFusion self-report framework. It is not a clinical or diagnostic tool and has not been independently validated. It is best used as a starting point for reflection and structured development, not as a measurement to be relied on alone. Details are on the methodology page.

Can NeuralFusion be used for hiring, selection or performance appraisal?

No. NeuralFusion is a development tool. The CFI should not be used as the sole basis for employment or other high-stakes decisions, and we do not recommend using it for hiring, promotion or performance ratings.

Can managers see an individual’s answers?

Team owners see how the team is doing, never anyone’s personal answers. Team results are aggregate and stay hidden until a minimum number of people have completed the assessment (five by default), so small groups do not expose individuals.

How does a team get started?

Create a team on the NeuralFusion team start page, name it and share one link. People who open the link create an account, join the team and land in the assessment. Seat limits are enforced on the server.

How long does the assessment take?

The 40-item CFI takes about eight minutes for most people.

What does it cost?

Seat-based team packages are shown on the team start page, so you can begin with the starter seats and choose a package when you need more. For anything custom, send an enquiry using the form on this page.

Do you offer facilitated or custom programmes?

We discuss facilitated delivery and custom arrangements individually. Use the enquiry form to describe your group and goals. Nothing is assumed in advance, and we will tell you plainly what is and is not available.
