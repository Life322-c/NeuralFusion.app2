Research at NeuralFusion™

Research at NeuralFusion™ explores how humans understand, develop, and preserve cognitive agency in an increasingly AI-mediated world.

NeuralFusion™ is an independent, emerging research and technology platform, not an academic institution. This page documents the founder's public research record and is kept separate from the platform's own proprietary frameworks, which are described on the methodology page.

What the publication examines

The publication examines human cognitive agency in the age of artificial intelligence: how people maintain independent judgment, structured reasoning, and ownership of their decisions as AI systems take on a growing share of the thinking work around them. This relates directly to NeuralFusion™'s broader focus on helping people understand and develop how they think.

How the research relates to NeuralFusion™

The relationship between this research and the NeuralFusion™ platform is intentional but specific. The publication is not a validation study of NeuralFusion™'s assessment methodology. It is the intellectual foundation the founder's thinking on human cognitive agency grew from, which in turn shaped the philosophy behind the platform's frameworks.
